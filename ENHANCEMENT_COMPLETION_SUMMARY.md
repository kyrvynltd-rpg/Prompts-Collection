### 2. **CRITICAL ISSUE RESOLUTION** ✅

#### Issue: Health & Wellness Liability Risk
- **Solution:** Added explicit medical disclaimers to all 8 health prompts
- **Examples:**
  - Fitness Planner: "Consult healthcare professionals before starting"
  - Mental Health Support: Includes crisis resources (988, Crisis Text Line)
  - Nutrition Advisor: "Consult registered dietitians for medical conditions"
  - Meditation Guide: "Consult mental health professionals for PTSD/psychosis"

#### Issue: Duplicate Sleep Prompts
- **Solution:** Removed generic "Sleep Optimization Advisor" (score 5.2/10)
- **Kept:** Better "Sleep Optimization Coach" (score 7.8/10)

#### Issue: Generic AI Assistant Category
- **Solution:** Replaced 1 generic "General Purpose Assistant" with 4 specialized versions:
  - General Purpose Assistant (rewritten with specificity)
  - Academic Research Assistant (new)
  - Market Research Specialist (new)
  - Competitive Research Analyst (new)

#### Issue: Customer Support Redundancy
- **Solution:** Differentiated with clear scope separation:
  - **Support Agent:** General troubleshooting, step-by-step
  - **Complaint Handler:** Emotional complaints, root cause analysis
