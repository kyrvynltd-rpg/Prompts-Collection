# Academic integrity and AI use

This product teaches students to use AI as a study aid, not as a ghost writer.

## Design rules for every Student Success prompt

1. **Learning first.** Prompts ask for plans, feedback, hints and questions. None of them write an assessed piece of work for the student.
2. **Honest by default.** Where a model could invent facts (references, statistics, experience), the prompt tells it not to and tells the student to verify.
3. **Student's own sources.** Quiz, flashcard and synthesis prompts work only from material the student pastes in.
4. **Policy beats product.** Every prompt carries an `integrity_note`. Students must follow their module and university AI policy, which can be stricter than this tool.
5. **No detection evasion.** The AI Use Declaration Helper helps write an accurate declaration. It will not help hide AI use.

## What the product does not claim

- It does not guarantee any grade, and it is not legal, medical or financial advice.
- It is not affiliated with any university.
- It cannot check what a specific institution allows. Students should ask their module leader when unsure.
